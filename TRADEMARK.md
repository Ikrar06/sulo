# Name, Logo and Mascot Policy

The source code of Sulo is licensed under the [MIT License](LICENSE). The Sulo name, logo, wordmark, flame icon and mascot are not covered by that license.

## What is covered

The brand assets in `assets/brand/`, including:
- the flame icon
- the lockups with the "SULO" wordmark
- the app icons in `public/icon/`
- the mascot and its expressions

The name "Sulo" when used as the name of a browser extension or study tool.

## What you may do

- Use the assets unchanged to refer to Sulo itself, for example in an article, a review or a link to this project.
- Keep the assets in a fork that you use privately or for development, and in pull requests to this repository.

## What you may not do

- Publish a modified or derived extension under the name Sulo, or with the Sulo logo or mascot. If you publish a fork, rename it and replace the brand assets, so that nobody mistakes it for Sulo.
- Use the assets in a way that suggests endorsement by the Sulo project, or by Universitas Hasanuddin.

## Fonts

The bundled fonts, Inter and Plus Jakarta Sans, are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The licenses are in `assets/fonts/inter/OFL.txt` and `assets/fonts/plus-jakarta-sans/OFL.txt`.

## University identity

Sulo is unofficial and is not affiliated with Universitas Hasanuddin. The project does not use, and contributions must not add, logos, colors or names that resemble the university's official identity. SIKOLA and NEOSIA are named only to describe where data comes from.

Questions about this policy can be raised in an issue.
